BarisKeser-ByeDPI bypasses Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) and censorship without a remote server.

It runs the open-source ByeDPI engine as a local SOCKS5 proxy on your device and, in VPN mode, routes your traffic through it using Android's on-device VPN. Nothing is sent to a remote VPN server, your traffic is not proxied off-device, and your IP address is not hidden or changed — the app only alters how the first packets of a connection are sent so DPI systems cannot easily block them.

Features:
• VPN mode (system-wide) or local SOCKS5 proxy mode
• Ready-made presets for Turkish ISPs (Türk Telekom, Turkcell Superonline, Kablonet, Vodafone) plus a general profile
• Advanced command-line editor for custom ByeDPI strategies (split, disorder, fake, TTL, TLS record split, and more)
• Configurable DNS and IPv6 in VPN mode
• Quick Settings tile to toggle on/off
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking

Privacy:
The app collects no personal data. All processing happens on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

BarisKeser-ByeDPI is free and open source. Engine: https://github.com/hufrea/byedpi
